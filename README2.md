# The main focus
**RTGs**!!!!
This mod adds different kinds of RTGs with special properties. (These RTGs use fuel btw)
# Fuels!
## Thorium Fuels
These are mainly combinations of Thorium with the following:
- Uranium - 4 - 4.5/10 power \(Depends on isotope)
- Plutonium - 3.5 - 6/10 power \(Depends on isotope)
- Neptunium - 7/10 power
- Curium - 6 - 8/10 power \(Depends on isotope)
- Californium - 9/10 power
- Berkilium - 7/10 power
- Americium - 8/10 power
Also the power depends on the amount of thorium. Low thorium, High [OTHER COMPOUND\] -> High energy. Half and Half -> Medium energy. High Thorium, Low [OTHER COMPOUND\] -> Low energy
\(A full list of each fuel and its power is in [FUELS.md])
