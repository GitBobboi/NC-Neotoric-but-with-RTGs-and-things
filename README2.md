# The main focus
**RTGs**!!!!
This mod adds different kinds of RTGs with special properties. (These RTGs use fuel btw)
# Fuels!
## Thorium Fuels
These are mainly combinations of Thorium with the following:
- Uranium - 4 - 4.5/10 power \(Depends on isotope)
- Plutonium - 3.5 - 6/10 power \(Depends on isotope)
- Neptunium - 7/10 power
- Curium - 6 - 8/10 power \(Depends on isotope)
- Californium - 9/10 power
- Berkilium - 7/10 power
- Americium - 8/10 power
Also the power depends on the amount of thorium. Low thorium, High [OTHER COMPOUND\] -> High energy. Half and Half -> Medium energy. High Thorium, Low [OTHER COMPOUND\] -> Low energy
\(A full list of each fuel and its power is in [FUELS.md](FUELS.md))
# Explaining what heat does and what emmisive flux is
## Emmisive Flux
This is how much flux the fuel naturally emits as it spews out electrons when is decays into bithmuth, lead, while going through all the stages.
## Heat
This is how much heat it produces, when heat > 150 it boils water and when its greater than 1500 it blows up with the force of 2 TNTs
### Ways to remove heat:
- Have it boil water, 1 B cools down 1000H, so 1 mB = 1H of cooling
- Use heatsinks, Im planning only to add glowstone, redstone, water, iron, lead, copper, and nickel coolers. each with different cooling rates. They dont make power, so only use them if the water is insignificant for whatever fuels you're using. btw they only activate at 500 just so that you dont miss out on all the boiling if you are supplying enough water.
- Break it, but its not recommended as it will blow up if H > 300 with the force of 4 TNTs
- Wait. When fuel isnt being proccessed and there is no water to boil, it will simply loose heat over time, very slowly though.
## Duration
Just how long it lasts. something with 200t of duraction will last 200 ticks in MC, or about 10 seconds if the tick rate is perfect (Depends on lag\)
